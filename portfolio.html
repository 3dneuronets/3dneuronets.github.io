Our core domains

Where science and computation converge.

3D NeuroNets brings together experimental neural data, biophysical modeling and artificial intelligence to investigate biological neural systems across scales.

Neural Data Analysis

Extract insights from electrophysiology and neural connectivity.

Biophysical Modeling

01 / Experimental data

Neural Data Analysis

Extract meaningful insights from experimental recordings and connectivity data, from individual cells to interacting neural populations.

Multi-Electrode Arrays (MEA)

Analyze multichannel recordings to characterize spiking, bursting and network-wide activity.

Whole-Cell Patch Clamp

Characterize membrane properties, action potentials and synaptic responses from electrophysiological recordings.

In Vivo Recordings

Extract neural activity patterns, oscillations and synchrony from recordings in living systems.

Functional & Anatomical Networks

Reconstruct and characterize connectivity using activity-derived and structural data.

02 / Computational models

Data-Driven Modeling

Use experimentally informed models to investigate how cellular mechanisms and circuit connectivity shape neural activity in health and disease.

Rodent Thalamocortical Models

Explore thalamocortical interactions, rhythms and sensory processing.

Rodent Hippocampal Models

Simulate hippocampal circuitry, synaptic interactions and population dynamics.

Epilepsy Modeling

Investigate mechanisms of hyperexcitability, seizure generation and pathological oscillations.

Aging & Neural Dynamics

Study how age-related changes in cellular and synaptic properties affect network function.

Ion Channel Models

Develop biophysical models of ionic currents, channel kinetics and membrane excitability.

Human Temporal Cortex Models

Integrate human neuronal data and connectivity into detailed cortical circuit simulations.

tACS & TMS Modeling

Model neuronal and network responses to extracellular electric fields and non-invasive brain stimulation.
