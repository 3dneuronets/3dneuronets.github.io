About 3D NeuroNets

Connecting neuroscience
and computation.

3D NeuroNets LLC develops computational solutions for neuroscience research and neurotechnology. We bring together experimental neural data, biophysically detailed simulations and artificial intelligence to investigate the brain across scales.

Led by computational neuroscientist Fernando da Silva Borges, Ph.D., we focus on translating scientific questions into quantitative, reproducible computational workflows.
