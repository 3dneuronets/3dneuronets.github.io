Who we are

Bridging experimental neuroscience and computational modeling.

3D NeuroNets LLC focuses on computational neuroscience and neurotechnology. Our aim is to help researchers and technology teams turn complex neural recordings and biological information into interpretable models, quantitative insights and computational experiments.

Our approach combines data analysis with mechanistic modeling and machine learning, linking cellular properties to neural circuit dynamics. We emphasize scientific grounding, reproducible workflows and solutions tailored to the research question.

Experimental grounding

Use biological observations to inform analysis and model design.

Multiscale modeling

Connect ion channels, neurons, synapses and interacting brain circuits.

Reproducible computation

Make methods transparent, testable and reusable wherever possible.

Meet our CEO.

Chief Executive Officer · 3D NeuroNets LLC

Fernando da Silva Borges, Ph.D.

Fernando is a computational neuroscientist trained in physics and biophysics. His research explores how neuronal properties and connectivity give rise to collective brain activity.

He earned his Ph.D. in Sciences at the State University of Ponta Grossa (UEPG), including a research period at the University of Aberdeen in Scotland. His postdoctoral research spanned the University of São Paulo, the Federal University of ABC and SUNY Downstate Health Sciences University.

His academic work includes large-scale, biologically detailed neural circuit models and the integration of computational and experimental neuroscience. He serves as a professor in UEPG’s Graduate Program in Sciences and a Research Scientist Consultant at SUNY Downstate.
